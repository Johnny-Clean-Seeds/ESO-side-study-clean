Opening

This reader opens the dossier, not the workshop.

The story begins with Hall's idea that America was not merely a place on a map but an assignment: a symbolic project with older religious, political, and mythic currents moving beneath it. Cooper carries that reading forward in audio. Mercy Hunter turns it into a visual problem: maps, fragments, symbols, and a blue-line image that seems to compress several worlds at once.

This site is where that work is shaped into chapters. The reader gets the story, the source routes, and the images that help the thread breathe.

The map problem

The blue map is a story object.

Mercy Hunter's map is best read here as a layered symbolic compression image: part archaeology trail, part Hall vocabulary, part video edit, and part theological ending. The page treats it as a reading model, not a solved historical claim or finished statement of editor intent.
