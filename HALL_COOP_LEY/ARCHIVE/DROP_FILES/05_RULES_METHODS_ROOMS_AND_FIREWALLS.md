## Claim-type classifier

`SOURCE-TEXT TEST` — what a source says.

`EDITOR-SEQUENCE TEST` — what the video appears to show or argue.

`SYMBOLIC-GRAMMAR TEST` — whether symbols share structured grammar.

`ARCHAEOLOGICAL-FIELD TEST` — whether a field is real in archaeology.

`MECHANISM TEST` — how a symbol functions as emblem, power-channel, path, map, office, or center.

`LINEAGE TEST` — whether one tradition/object/symbol descends from another.

`IDENTITY TEST` — whether two systems are the same institution.

`CAUSATION TEST` — whether one thing caused another.

Lineage, identity, and causation require the hardest proof.
